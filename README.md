## 01 / The person behind the signal

**I'm Benedict. I build across the space between a model, a microcontroller, and the person using the product.**

I'm an Electronics Engineering student at **PUP Sta. Mesa** and an **AI & Edge AI Product Integrator at Loompoint AI**. My work moves between applied ML, full-stack software, and embedded systems: getting an idea out of the notebook and into the conditions where it actually has to work.

The question that keeps me interested: **the model works. Does the product?**

Away from the keyboard, I make portraits and landscapes. That part of me stays at the workbench, too: observe closely, find the structure, keep refining.

<summary>About the signal sculpture</summary>

The contour in the cover is drawn from a seed made from **TENSORKAI / BENEDICT FUSIN**. Its nested paths bring together a signal trace, a circuit, and the contour lines of a drawing. The small marks beneath it encode `TENSORKAI` in binary.

All artwork lives in this repository. The SVGs use gentle CSS animation, respect reduced-motion preferences, and remain complete as still images. To regenerate them, run `node scripts/generate-art.mjs`.
